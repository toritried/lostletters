If I were newer to the stationery and journaling hobby, I'd likely buy the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_toolstoys/tt_inkset_tsuki/">Hobonichi-colored mini ink samples</a>, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_toolstoys/tt_bookband_kitagishi/">Yumi Kitagishi book band</a>, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_cover/cc27_bpeople/">beautiful people: Nothing to Hide (Pink) A5 cover</a>, and <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_toolstoys/tt_clip_kitagishi/">Yumi Kitagishi clips</a> from the 2027 Hobonichi lineup, but I'm <a href="/journaling/#my-2022-no-buy-year">years into reining in my overconsumption of stationery</a>, so I am very good at justifying why I shouldn't get these items beyond simply balking at the price.<!--excerpt-->

Like most reasonable people, I was scandalized by the $55 price tag for the mini ink samples that match the Hobonichi colors. I yearn for these inks even though I won't be purchasing a Hobonichi Techo or Cousin for 2027, so I got inspired by <a target="_blank" href="https://www.reddit.com/r/hobonichi/comments/1vyesj5/kakimori_inks_vs_the_matching_colors_i_already/">this reddit post of someone swatching their fountain pen inks</a> and <a target="'_blank" href="https://www.jetpens.com/blog/The-Best-Pens-and-Accessories-for-the-Hobonichi-Techo/pt/335">this guide from JetPens of matching pens and inks for the Hobonichi</a> to shop my stash of colored pens for matching inks before making a purchase.

<img style="margin: auto; display: block;" src="/graphics/blog/2026/hobonichi-ink-swatches.jpg" alt="12 ink swatches of various colors in from uniball one and zebra sarasa pens" title="my attempt at matching the Hobonichi colors with pens I have in my collection">

When I lived in Japan, I couldn't resist buying packs of limited edition Uniball One pens, so I ended up with 60 different colors in my collection. I really regretted committing to the 0.38mm size instead of the smoother 0.5mm because the pigment-based ink tends to skip in the 0.38mm pens. However, I already own these, and they are hardly used, so I figured I'd make the best of the situation and try to find ink dupes. What I couldn't match with my Uniball One pens, I tried to match with my 0.5mm Zebra Sarasa Clip Vintage Gel Pens. I'm pretty happy with how close I got with my existing pen collection, and swatching these definitely helped dismiss my need for the official inks. I'm not even sure I'll use them given I prefer how my print writing looks with 0.5mm pens better than 0.38mm, but if you're similarly on the fence about the cost performance of these inks, consider shopping your stash too!

The <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_toolstoys/tt_bookband_kitagishi/">Yumi Kitagishi book band</a> was more difficult to avoid purchasing as I don't have anything like it in my collection of stationery tools. However, with my local stationery store is selling it for almost double the original price listed on the Hobonichi website, so I knew I was not going to justify that one. Also, I plan to reuse an old hobonichi A5 cover for my 2027 planner, so it wouldn't fit around the cover. Since I already have a usable A5 cover, I probably shouldn't splurge on such an expensive ($155) plastic <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_cover/cc27_bpeople/">beautiful people: Nothing to Hide (Pink) cover</a> no matter how good that transparent pink color looks. 

<img style="margin: auto; display: block;" src="/graphics/blog/2026/tama-shaker-clips.png" alt="a promo ad of the five different shaker clips from Tamagotchi" title="Tamagotchi shaking acrylic clips">

Finally, I already have the full series of large Tamagotchi-themed clips from a gachapon release, so I really don't need the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/2027/pc/detail_toolstoys/tt_clip_kitagishi/">Yumi Kitagishi clips</a> for my notebooks. I never would have remembered I owned these if it weren't for being incredibly aggressive when giving away items in preparation for my international move almost two years ago. I was left with items I truly love, and I now use them way more often than when my collection was out of control. If you don't have the capacity to do a big purge of stationery, then I recommend going through your items and creating a capsule of stationery products you're excited about that, paying particular attention to which items you already own that can fill the same need as the items on your wish list.
