For the <a target="_blank" href="https://myrtletribe.neocities.org/potluck-2026/announcement/">Small Web Virtual Potluck</a>, I wanted to stick to the optional theme this year: Yellow! After brainstorming all the yellow foods I enjoy eating, I figured that the easiest thing that wouldn't require me to bake would be incorporating pineapple and turmeric into something.  This led me towards a sweet and savory satay idea.<!--excerpt--> I don't know if this is technically considered satay because I didn't skewer it nor grill over an open flame as I made do with a pan. Anyways, feast your eyes on this quite yellow vegan meal!

<img style="margin: auto; display: block;" src="/graphics/blog/2026/tofu-satay.png" alt="a hand holding a dish of tofu and pineapple covered in peanut sauce over rice" title="Vegan Pineapple Tofu Satay">

I loved the sweet seared pineapple mixed with the savory peanut sauce, and I was impressed by how much a 15 minute marinade could imbue flavor into pretty sizable tofu chunks. This dish was definitely missing a vegetable though. Some steamed broccoli would have elevated it, and even some onion chunks would have been greatly appreciated. I would have preferred the tofu grilled to get some char and crispiness, but it was too hot to grill today and would have taken a lot more in terms of clean up than I was willing to do. All in all, I would rate this a 6 out of 10 for a recipe that I'm trying out for the first time, which isn't that bad. The original recipe I followed to make this is <a target="_blank" href="https://debraklein.com/tofu-satay-recipe/#recipe">Tofu Satay by Debra Klein</a>. I think Debra did a pretty great job flavor-wise, but I couldn't help but modify some of the ingredients and included pineapple and rice. The following is my slightly modified version of her recipe:
### Ingredients | Serves: 4 portions
<ul>
	<li>1 block of extra-firm tofu, dried & cubed</li>
	<li>1.5 cups of pineapple chunks, cut to a similar size as your tofu cubes</li>
	<li>1 rice cooker cup of rice</li>
	<li>Marinade
		<ul>
				<li>3 Tbsp Soy Sauce</li>
				<li>Juice of half a lime</li>
				<li>1 Tbsp maple syrup</li>
				<li>1 tsp ground turmeric</li>
				<li>A dash of cracked black pepper</li>
				<li>4 cloves of garlic, pressed</li>
				<li>1 Tbsp of minced fresh ginger</li>
			</ul>
		</li>
	<li>Peanut Sauce
		<ul>
				<li>¼ cup peanut butter</li>
				<li>1 Tbsp rice vinegar</li>
				<li>1 Tbsp soy sauce</li>
				<li>1 Tbsp maple syrup</li>
				<li>1 tsp of minced fresh ginger</li>
				<li>A dash of red pepper flakes to taste</li>
				<li>Juice from the other half of the lime</li>
				<li>1-2 Tbsp water to thin until preferred consistency</li>
			</ul>
		</li>
</ul>
### Cooking Instructions | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 20 min
<ul>
	<li>Start cooking your rice according to your rice cooker settings</li>
	<li>Prepare the marinade</li>
	<li>Let tofu marinate for 15 mins at least</li>
	<li>While the tofu is marinating, prepare the peanut sauce</li>
	<li>On a grill or in a pan, heat it to medium, then sear pineapple on all sides</li>
	<li>Once the tofu has marinated for 15 minutes or more, sear the tofu</li>
	<li>Serve warm with peanut sauce</li>
</ul>
