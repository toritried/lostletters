<h1 style="text-align:center;">Easy & Tasty Vegan Recipes</h1>
I started seriously cooking at the start of the pandemic when my office closed, cutting me off from vegan options at the cafeteria, at a point where my Japanese wasn't quite good enough to reliably order vegan food from restaurants. Although my Japanese improved considerably since then, I kept the habit of maintaining a large database of all the recipes I've attempted since then with notes on modifications as well as ratings of flavor and vibes-based nutrition scores. This page is a collection of recipes that helped me fall in love with cooking and ones I still spam for their nutritiousness and flavor. I hope you enjoy them!
<div class="recipe two-col">
    <h2>🍚 Tofu Fried Rice in a Rice Cooker</h2>
    <p>
        Original recipe credit: <a target="_blank" href="https://cookingforpeanuts.com/one-pot-rice-cooker-tofu-fried-rice/">Cooking for Peanuts</a>
    </p>
    <p>
        This has been in heavy rotation for those lazy work from home days. It takes about an hour for the rice cooker to make this meal, so you need to plan in advance a bit. It's convenient because it's mostly just pantry and freezer staples aside from fresh tofu for protein, so it's very convenient.
    </p>
    <h3>Ingredients | Serves: 2 portions</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>1 rice cooker cup of white rice</li>
        <li>water or broth for 1 cup of white rice</li>
        <li>1 cup of frozen vegetable medley (peas, carrots, corn, green beans, etc.)</li>
        <li>5-6 dried shiitake mushroom caps, sliced thinly (according to taste)</li>
        <li>1/2 block of tofu (medium or firmer), cubed</li>
        <li>ginger, grated if fresh or powdered, according to taste</li>
        <li>4 cloves of garlic, pressed</li>
        <li>green onions to taste, reserve some for topping at the end</li>
        <li>1 tbsp rice vinegar</li>
        <li>2 tbsp soy sauce</li>
        <li>1 tsp sesame oil</li>
        <li>2 tsp maple syrup</li>
        <li>2 tsp sriracha</li>
        <li>dash of onion powder</li>
        <li>dash of white pepper</li>
        <li>Toppings:
            <ul>
                <li>toasted sesame seeds for topping</li>
                <li>chili crisp for topping if that's your thing</li>
            </ul>
        </li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Cooking Instructions | Prep: 5 min & Cook: 1 hour</h3>
    <ol>
        <li>Wash rice</li>
        <li>Add all the ingredients to a pot aside from the toppings</li>
        <li>After cooking, add toppings</li>
    </ol>
</div>
<div class="recipe two-col">
    <h2>🥘 Gānguō (干锅) Dry Pot Stir Fry</h2>
    <p>
        Original recipe credit: <a target="_blank" href="https://youtu.be/S6YXm8dTHt0">Chinese Cooking Demystified</a>
    </p>
    <p>
        This quick meal is another one that uses mostly pantry and freezer staples, so it's quite easy to make provided you have some Chinese spices in your cabinet. This recipe is designed for ~1 kg ingredients, so it makes quite a bit of food, but it's really tasty even for leftovers.
    </p>
    <h3>Ingredients | Serves: 4+ portions</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Main Ingredients:
            <ul>
                <li>1 small bag of frozen stir fry veg (sugar snap peas, bell pepper, mushrooms, broccoli florets, etc.)</li>
                <li>1 small bag of frozen potato wedges</li>
                <li>1 block or equivalent of fried tofu, cubed</li>
            </ul>
        </li>
        <li>Sauce Ingredients:
            <ul>
                <li>2 tbsp veg oil</li>
                <li>4 cloves of garlic, pressed</li>
                <li>1/2 tbsp peanut butter</li>
                <li>2.5 tbsp hoisin sauce</li>
                <li>2 tsp sriracha</li>
                <li>1 tbsp soy sauce</li>
            </ul>
        </li>
        <li>Spice Mix:
            <ul>
                <li>1 tsp five spice powder</li>
                <li>.5 tsp salt</li>
                <li>.5 tsp sugar</li>
                <li>.5 bouillon powder</li>
                <li>1 tsp white pepper</li>
            </ul>
        </li>
        <li>Toppings:
            <ul>
                <li>1/2 red onion, chopped</li>
                <li>cilantro to taste</li>
            </ul>
        </li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Cooking Instructions | Prep: 5 min & Cook: 20 min</h3>
    <ol>
        <li>Start air frying the potato wedges, 15-20 mins typically</li>
        <li>Microwave frozen veggies until defrosted, 3-5 mins typically</li>
        <li>Make Sauce
            <ol>
                <li>Over a medium heat, quickly fry the garlic in the oil until fragrant, ~30 seconds.</li>
                <li>Add the peanut butter and mix</li>
                <li>Fry until foamy and slightly darkened, ~6-7 minutes.</li>
                <li>Add the hoisin, quick mix to combine</li>
                <li>Add the sriracha, quick mix to combine</li>
                <li>Add the soy sauce, cook for 1-2 minutes to expel some moisture. </li>
                <li>Add in spice mix, quick fry.</li>
            </ol>
        </li>
        <li>Bring it all together
            <ol>
                <li>Fry 2 tbsp oil + 2 cloves chopped garlic for 30 seconds</li>
                <li>Add in sauce, fry until foaming and slightly darkened, ~6-7 minutes.</li>
                <li>Add all main ingredients and mix thoroughly</li>
                <li>Add in seasoning and mix again</li>
                <li>Add half a chopped red onion and cilantro</li>
            </ol>
        </li>
    </ol>
</div>
