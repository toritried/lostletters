<b>CW: scene spoilers</b>

Just like the first book in the series, this biopunk story filled with political and court intrigue delivers a wonderful mystery adventure that's solved through clues woven from delightfully rich world building. I liked that we were transported to a very different part of the realm, one that instantly felt palpable and evocative. Malo was a fabulous side character that enriched the story and played off Din well.

The Din and Ana dynamic continues to deliver and delight as Din puts together clues himself instead of solely relying on Ana's great mind to solve the mystery for the reader. I really liked the reveal at the end, explaining Ana's origins and mental powers, but the part from this book that stood out to me the most was the Shroud scene where several augurs were talking at once. The voices and delivery by the audiobook narrator for that scene were fantastic, and then the author also did a wonderful job of creating something akin to an eerie hive mind with slightly different characterizations for each person to make them stand out. Masterfully done!

What's neglected is the development of the main character, Din. In this book, we learn that he's adept at bedding women (in addition to men from the first book), but it's almost covered as an afterthought. He has only two main aspirations that are explained in a bit more detail here, but we don't get much more than that.
